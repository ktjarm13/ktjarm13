## Feeding Liverpool Campaigns Concepts

Title: Feeding Liverpool Campaigns Concepts

Subtitle: Initial Thoughts on Digital and Other Campaigns

3 sections. 

### Section One

#### Text

Liverpool’s Good Food Stories  
What  
Community Storytelling \- Amplifying community kitchens, grassroots organisations and local champions  
Why

1. We have many organisations working hard who deserve recognition  
2. The Silver award for Sustainable Food Places requires ‘demonstrating genuine representation and justice’  
3. I believe if people see what is happening at these places they may feel less intimidated and be more likely to use the service and understand it can be for them.

How  
Tea with the Queen: A weekly video where we cook a meal with the ingredients likely to be in the Queen of Greens van this week. Menu cards are then available on the Bus and online we encourage others to share their versions with \#TeaWithTheQueen.

How  
Spotlight: Every organisation in the group should have spotlights of: volunteers, organisers and service users. Shared on Social Media and then available on the website (more on this in concept three)

#### Links

Menu cards should link to a page called ‘Recipes’ with placeholder for image and text

### Section Two

#### Text

Food Rights in Action  
What  
Rally residents and stakeholders around Phase Two of the Good Food Plan.  
Why  
It’s important to shift the narrative from just short term crisis response to systemic changes to food rights and justice on a local level.  
How  
Establish the LCR Food Council: I’ve been a keen member of the Merseyside Trans+ council. Several groups who work to support the trans+ community get together to understand where the gaps are and where any overlap is. I would seek to set up something similar to this for food sustainability and justice. This group could then work together to support cross referrals, funding applications and other support.

Alleyway Gardens: Work with neighbourhoods to clean up alleyways and create alley food gardens growing food and other plants for pollinators. This could go on to include friendly competitions. This gives people not only access to fresh food but encourages them to care more about where they live.

### Section Three

#### Text

 Mapping & Evidencing Good Food Liverpool  
What  
Transform Feeding Liverpool’s existing digital directory from a static map into an interactive impact map and data engine.   
Why  
While residents can currently find the support, it would be good to also gather, visualize and communicate the ‘added value’ as this is a key part of Silver Sustainable Food Places accreditation.   
How  
When clicking on the different places on the food spaces map along with the existing link and information the ‘Spotlights’ from concept one are shown along with certain stats, such as the amount of people supported that month.  
**Capturing Collective Impact Metrics:** Integrate lightweight reporting fields into the map portal (or partner check-ins) to track metrics that prove partnership synergy—e.g., *number of meals redirected from waste, cross-referrals between health/social services and food hubs, volunteer hours mobilized, and co-located advice services offered*.
